Details

Asked often
Why do prices say "starting from"?

Because a three-product launch and a fifty-SKU catalogue are different jobs. Every proposal is scoped to your brand and market — the figure shown is the honest floor for your region, typically well below comparable agency quotes.

Do you build on Shopify?

Yes — our own brand runs on it. We design and build on Shopify and comparable platforms, and we hand over a store your team can operate without us.

How long does a build take?

Most stores go live within four to six weeks of approving the concept. The Programme and The Partnership then run on a monthly rhythm.

Can the imagery programme run on an existing store?

Yes. If your store already works, The Programme can run standalone — monthly imagery, campaigns and content in a locked visual language, without a rebuild.

How does an engagement begin?

Write to badal@sutrahaus.com with your brand's name. We study your store and channels, then present a complimentary concept — your homepage, reimagined — before any commitment.
