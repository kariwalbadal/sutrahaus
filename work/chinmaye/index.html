Sutra Haus / Work / Chinmaye

A landmark hotel, finally looking like one.

Hotel Chinmaye Inn — 60 rooms, a rooftop restaurant and five banquet halls in Bhagalpur, Bihar — had a website that undersold every one of them. We rebuilt the whole digital presence: two websites, a direct-booking flow, and 125+ creatives in a visual language invented just for Chinmaye.

The problemA generic template with stock icons, slow pages and "Welcome to our hotel" copy — while OTAs took a commission on every booking it deserved to win directly.
The rebuildEditorial design on the hotel's real night photography, Hinglish copy with Bhagalpuri warmth, five banquet halls given their own stage, careers page, and a booking flow that keeps guests on-property.
The machinerySTAAH booking engine integrated. A live rate strip compares the direct price against five OTAs — refreshed automatically three times a day — currently proving direct is ~30% cheaper.

The Feed

125+ creatives. Zero clip-art.

Bhagalpur's hotel Instagram was a sea of clip-art festival posters. So we invented a language only Chinmaye speaks — cricket scoreboards counting wedding guests, panditji's calendar, a newspaper front page, mithai boxes with one laddoo reserved for the booking. Every piece hand-built, layer by layer.
