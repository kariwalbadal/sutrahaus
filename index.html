06 · Questions

Asked often, answered honestly.

Do you only work with hotels?

Hotels, resorts and banquet venues are our specialty — but we're a full media haus. We've built complete brands and e-commerce stores too, like Ephoria. If your business needs a premium website and creatives, we can help.

What does a rebuild include?

Everything: bespoke design, full build, booking-engine integration, live OTA rate comparison, WhatsApp & click-to-call, SEO and AI-search readiness, plus managed hosting, domain and SSL. See the Chinmaye case study for exactly what that looks like.

How fast will the new site be?

Speed is engineered in, not hoped for. Our recent hotel rebuild scores in the 90s on Google Lighthouse mobile tests — self-hosted fonts, optimized images, instant first paint.

Can guests still book through our existing engine?

Yes. We integrate your existing booking engine (STAAH and others) into the new site — and we can show guests live proof that booking direct is cheaper than OTAs.

Do you use templates or generic AI designs?

Never. Templates are the cardinal sin here. Every pixel is designed from zero for your brand — nothing we make for one client is ever reused for another.

How do we start?

Email badal@sutrahaus.com with your property's name. We'll study your current website and Instagram, then show you your homepage reimagined — free, before you commit to anything.
