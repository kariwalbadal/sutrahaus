The studio

One thread through everything.

Sutra — the Sanskrit word for thread: the single line that holds a story together. Haus — the studio that weaves it. The name is a promise of coherence: your website, your imagery and your film, made by the same hands, in the same language.

Sutra Haus is the studio of Sutrahaus LLP, Bengaluru — owner-led and deliberately small. The person you write to is the person who designs, builds and ships the work: no account managers, no hand-offs, no templates. Our first works — a landmark hotel's complete digital rebuild and a consumer brand built from zero — are both live, and both proof of the same conviction: premium is the feeling three seconds after the page loads.
