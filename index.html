The studio

One thread through everything.

Sutra — the Sanskrit word for thread: the single line that holds a story together. Haus — the studio that weaves it. The name is a promise of coherence: your website, your imagery and your film, made by the same hands, in the same language.

Sutra Haus is owner-led. The person you write to is the person who designs, builds and ships the work — no account managers, no hand-offs, no templates. The studio's first works — a landmark hotel's complete digital rebuild and a consumer brand built from zero — are both live and both proof of the same conviction: premium is not a price tag, it is the feeling three seconds after the page loads.

Details

Asked often
Who does the studio work with?

Hotels, resorts and hospitality groups — from independent properties to international chains — and consumer brands that care how they're seen. We work remotely, worldwide, with on-property time arranged where the work needs it.

What does a website engagement include?

A complete redesign and build: bespoke design, fast mobile-first engineering, booking integration, search and AI-search readiness, and managed hosting with the domain and content always owned by you. Recent hotel work holds Lighthouse mobile scores in the nineties.

Can we keep our existing booking engine?

Yes. We integrate your current engine into the new website, and can surface a live comparison of your direct rates against OTA prices — a quiet, permanent argument for booking direct.
